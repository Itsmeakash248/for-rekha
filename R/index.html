chapter two · the weight of it

I was sharp with you
and you went quiet.

Tuesday, the kitchen. You stopped mid-sentence and I kept talking. I saw it land. I didn’t stop. That silence has followed me since — I’m not here to explain it away. I’m here to carry it properly.

No excuses in this chapter. Only the fact of it.

That evening,

I keep returning to the same few seconds. Drag through them with me. Scroll back to rewind — I wish I could.

chapter five · what changes

Not grand gestures.
Small things, kept.

Five concrete promises. Each one has its own flower, because each one I intend to tend separately.

01

I listen to the end.

No phone in hand when you’re telling me something that matters. Eyes up, mouth shut until you’re done.

02

Overwhelmed means pause, not snap.

When work boils over I’ll say “I need twenty minutes” instead of letting it leak onto you.

03

No hard talks after eleven.

Tired words bruise. If it’s late, we sleep and return kind in the morning. Your rest is sacred.

04

Sundays are yours.

Phone in a drawer from breakfast to dinner. Walk, cook, nothing productive required. Just us.

05

I ask, I don’t assume.

“How can I help?” before fixing. “What do you need?” before deciding. Every time, even when I think I know.

chapter six · resolution

Morning coffee, no screens, your head on my shoulder.
The argument we repair faster because we learned this one.
A home that feels soft to land in.
